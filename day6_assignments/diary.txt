Income tax in India is a direct tax levied by the government on the annual income earned by individuals, businesses, and other entities.
Tax Regimes and SlabsIndia has two tax systems: the Old Tax Regime (which permits many deductions and exemptions) and the New Tax Regime (which offers lower tax rates but fewer deductions).
