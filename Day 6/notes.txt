Generative AI (GenAI) is a type ofartificial intelligence that can create new content such as text, images, code, audio, and videos.
It learns patterns from large amounts of existing data and uses them to generate new, human-like content.
Examples include ChatGPT, Google Gemini, GitHub Copilot, and DALL·E.
In simple terms, traditional AI analyzes or predicts; GenAI creates.
